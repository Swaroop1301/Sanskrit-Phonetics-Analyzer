🎓 Learn: Sanskrit Phonetics

स्वर — Vowels

Vowels (स्वर) are sounds produced without any obstruction to the airflow. Sanskrit recognises 13 pure vowels plus अं (Anusvāra) and अः (Visarga).

  • हृस्व (Short): अ, इ, उ, ऋ, ऌ
  • दीर्घ (Long): आ, ई, ऊ, ॠ, ॡ, ए, ऐ, ओ, औ

व्यञ्जन — Consonants

Consonants (व्यञ्जन) are produced by partially or fully blocking the airflow. They are grouped into:

  • स्पर्श (Stops): क–म (25 consonants, 5 vargas)
  • अन्तस्थ (Semi-vowels): य, र, ल, व
  • ऊष्म (Sibilants): श, ष, स, ह

उच्चारण स्थान — Places of Articulation

Every Sanskrit sound is defined by where in the mouth it is produced:

Place Sanskrit English Examples
कण्ठ कण्ठ्य Guttural (Throat) अ, आ, क-वर्ग, ह, विसर्ग
तालु तालव्य Palatal (Hard palate) इ, ई, च-वर्ग, य, श
मूर्धा मूर्धन्य Retroflex (Roof of mouth) ऋ, ट-वर्ग, र, ष
दन्त दन्त्य Dental (Teeth) ऌ, त-वर्ग, ल, स
ओष्ठ ओष्ठ्य Labial (Lips) उ, ऊ, प-वर्ग

Combined places like कण्ठतालु (ए, ऐ), कण्ठोष्ठ (ओ, औ), and दन्तोष्ठ (व) also exist.
